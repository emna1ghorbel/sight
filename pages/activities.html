IOT in Smart Farming

IoT in Smart Farming", organized in collaboration with IEEE SIGHT ENIS SBG and IEEE PES ENIS SBC, introduced participants to the role of IoT in modern agriculture. Led by Ms. Amina Kessentini, the session covered IoT and AI applications in energy, followed by an exploration of sensors, boards like the ESP32, and hands-on prototype building. Participants created smart farming models to monitor soil moisture, temperature, and light, gaining practical skills in coding, circuit design, and data collection. The event concluded with a collaborative project session, highlighting the potential of IoT to drive sustainable farming solutions.

AI and IoT in Modern Agriculture

Ms. Amina Kessentini conducted a session on the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) in agriculture. She highlighted how these emerging technologies are transforming the future of farming by enabling smarter real-time monitoring.

Turning Waste into Worth: A Journey Toward Sustainable Solutions

Led by Mr. Ramzi Halouani, the session offered insightful exploration into the global waste crisis and introduced innovative solutions for sustainable waste management. Emphasizing practices like reduction, recycling, and creative upcycling, the workshop inspired participants to embrace eco-conscious habits and take on active roles as sustainability advocates in their communities.

ESS RECY LAB PROJECT

ESS RECY LAB is an eco-conscious initiative launched with the support of IEEE ENIS SB, WIE ENIS, WIE ENET’COM, and PES, aiming to promote plastic recycling and sustainable innovation in the engineering community. Held on November 23, 2024, at Hotel Palais Royal, the event challenged students to design smart sorting devices called "Waste Wizards" to separate plastic and paper waste. The competition encouraged environmental responsibility, creativity, and hands-on learning. The IEEE ENIS Student Branch won the challenge with their standout prototype, demonstrating both innovation and commitment to sustainability.

Smart Farm

We organized the event "IoT in Smart Farming," in collaboration with IEEE PES, where participants explored the transformative role of IoT in agriculture. They were introduced to essential IoT components—including sensors, boards, and the ESP32—before diving into a hands-on workshop, where they built smart farming prototypes to monitor key environmental factors like soil moisture, temperature, and light. The event concluded with a collaborative project, where teams developed solutions to real farming challenges. Participants gained hands-on experience in coding, circuit design, and data collection, leaving inspired to innovate in sustainable agriculture.

TSYP12 Challenge (PES & RAS & SIGHT)

As part of the TSYP Technical Challenge, our team developed a Smart Waste Bin system aimed at optimizing waste collection through intelligent monitoring and data-driven decision-making. The project aligns with Tunisia’s environmental needs and the UN Sustainable Development Goals, focusing on cleaner, more sustainable cities. Backed by the humanitarian spirit of our IEEE SIGHT group, we ensured the solution was not only technically innovative but also socially impactful, making waste management more efficient in underserved communities and raising awareness about responsible waste practices. Our commitment to both technology and community impact earned us a place in the Top 3 winners of the challenge, proving that engineering can drive real change when guided by humanitarian values.
